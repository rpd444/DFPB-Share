RELEASE OF LIABILITY, ASSUMPTION OF RISK, AND RIDER AGREEMENT

Private Riding Track, Kittitas County, Washington

READ CAREFULLY BEFORE SIGNING. THIS AGREEMENT AFFECTS YOUR LEGAL RIGHTS. BY SIGNING IT, YOU GIVE UP THE RIGHT TO SUE THE RELEASED PARTIES FOR INJURY OR DEATH CAUSED BY THEIR ORDINARY NEGLIGENCE.

The short notes in italics explain each section in everyday language. They are for convenience only. If a note and the agreement text differ, the agreement text controls.

Owners: John Ware and Jessica Ware (together, the “Owners”). Property: 8112 4th Parallel Rd, Ellensburg, WA 98926, including the riding track, jumps, trails, and staging and spectator areas on it (the “Property”).

Rider: ______________________________ Date of birth: ____________ Phone: ________________

Emergency contact: ______________________________ Phone: ________________

Bike(s) the Rider will ride (make, model, electric or gas): ___________________________

1. Definitions. “Released Parties” means the Owners, the members of their household, and any adult assisting them in operating or supervising the track. “Activities” means riding, practicing, or operating any electric bicycle, dirt bike, or similar vehicle on the Property, and being present on or near the track or riding areas for any reason, on the date of this Agreement and on any later date it covers.

In plain terms: “Released Parties” is everyone this agreement protects: John, Jessica, their household (including their son), and any adult helping run the track.

2. Private, voluntary use. The Rider takes part in the Activities as a personal guest of the Owners, by invitation and without charge. The track is a private, non-commercial facility. It is not licensed, sanctioned, or inspected, and no medical personnel, flaggers, or safety officials are provided. Participation is voluntary.

In plain terms: This is a family track, not a business. Nobody inspects it, and no safety staff are on site. Riding is your choice.

3. Inherent risks. The Activities are inherently dangerous. The risks include, without limitation, falls; collisions with other riders, vehicles, persons, and fixed objects; being thrown from jumps, berms, ruts, and uneven ground; mechanical failure; changing weather and surface conditions; the conduct of other riders; delayed emergency response due to the rural location; and the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties in designing, building, maintaining, or supervising the track. These risks can cause serious bodily injury, including broken bones, head and spinal injuries, paralysis, and death, as well as property damage.

In plain terms: Riding is dangerous, and you can be badly hurt or killed. Some risks may come from the owners’ own mistakes in building or running the track.

4. Express assumption of risk. The Rider has read Section 3, understands the risks of the Activities, and knowingly and voluntarily assumes all of those risks, known and unknown, including risks arising from the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties. The Rider will inspect the track before riding, ride within the Rider’s ability, and stop riding and notify an Owner of any hazard the Rider observes.

In plain terms: You understand the risks and accept them, including the risk that the owners make a mistake.

5. RELEASE, WAIVER, AND PROMISE NOT TO SUE. In consideration of permission to use the Property, the Rider, for the Rider and the Rider’s heirs, personal representatives, and estate, RELEASES, WAIVES, AND DISCHARGES THE RELEASED PARTIES FROM, AND PROMISES NOT TO SUE THEM FOR, ANY AND ALL CLAIMS, DEMANDS, AND CAUSES OF ACTION FOR BODILY INJURY, DEATH, OR PROPERTY DAMAGE ARISING OUT OF OR RELATED TO THE ACTIVITIES, INCLUDING CLAIMS CAUSED BY THE ORDINARY NEGLIGENCE OF THE RELEASED PARTIES. This Section does not release claims for gross negligence or for willful, wanton, or intentional misconduct.

In plain terms: If you get hurt, you agree not to sue the owners, even if their carelessness caused it. This also binds your family and estate. It does not cover extreme carelessness or harm done on purpose.

6. Rules. The Rider will follow the Track Rules attached to this Agreement, which are part of it, and all instructions of the Owners. Any Owner may end any rider’s session at any time.

In plain terms: Follow the rules on the last page. The owners can stop anyone from riding.

7. Responsibility for the Rider’s own conduct. The Rider is responsible for the Rider’s own vehicle and equipment. To the extent permitted by Washington law, the Rider will indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from claims by third parties for injury or property damage caused by the Rider’s acts or omissions or by the Rider’s vehicle.

In plain terms: If you or your bike hurts someone else or damages their property, that is on you, not the owners.

8. Emergency care. If the Rider is injured and unable to consent, the Rider authorizes the Owners to call emergency services and to arrange first aid and medical transport. The Owners are not medical providers, have no duty to provide medical care, and are not responsible for the cost of any medical care or transport.

In plain terms: If you are hurt and can’t speak for yourself, the owners can call 911. They don’t provide or pay for medical care.

9. Term; general provisions. This Agreement applies to the date signed and to every later visit within 12 months after that date, unless the signer revokes it earlier by written notice to an Owner. It is governed by the laws of the State of Washington, and venue for any dispute lies in Kittitas County. This Agreement is intended to be as broad and inclusive as Washington law permits. If any part is held unenforceable, the remainder stays in full force and effect. This is the entire agreement on its subject, and it may be signed electronically or in counterparts.

In plain terms: This covers every visit for a year unless you cancel it in writing. Washington law applies. If a court throws out one part, the rest still counts.

RIDER UNDER 18: PARENT OR LEGAL GUARDIAN

A. Consent. I am the parent or legal guardian of the Rider and have legal authority to consent for the Rider. I consent to the Rider’s participation in the Activities. I have read this Agreement and agree to its terms for myself, and on the Rider’s behalf to the extent the law allows.

B. Parent’s own release. For myself and my estate, I RELEASE, WAIVE, AND DISCHARGE THE RELEASED PARTIES FROM, AND PROMISE NOT TO SUE THEM FOR, ALL CLAIMS I HAVE OR MAY HAVE ARISING OUT OF THE RIDER’S ACTIVITIES, INCLUDING CLAIMS FOR THE RIDER’S MEDICAL EXPENSES AND FOR LOSS OF THE RIDER’S SERVICES, LOVE, AND COMPANIONSHIP, AND INCLUDING CLAIMS CAUSED BY THE ORDINARY NEGLIGENCE OF THE RELEASED PARTIES. This does not release claims for gross negligence or for willful, wanton, or intentional misconduct.

C. Assumption of risk and supervision. I understand the risks in Section 3 and assume them for myself. I have determined that the Rider’s age, size, skill, and vehicle are appropriate for the track. I, or an adult I name below, will be present and supervising whenever the Rider is on the Property, and I will see that the Rider follows the Track Rules.

D. Medical consent. If I cannot be reached, I authorize emergency medical treatment and transport for the Rider.

In plain terms: You allow your child to ride and accept the risks. You give up your own right to sue (for example, for medical bills you pay). You promise that you or another adult you name will be there watching, and that your child will follow the rules.

TRACK RULES

These rules are part of the Agreement and are posted at the track.

1. Wear full gear every ride: a full-face motorcycle helmet (DOT or ECE certified), goggles, over-the-ankle boots, gloves, long pants, and long sleeves. A chest protector and knee braces are strongly recommended.

2. Anyone under 18 rides only when an adult (18 or older) is on site and supervising. That adult must be the parent or the adult the parent named on the form.

3. Never ride alone. At least one other person must be present.

4. Ride in one direction only. No passengers. No racing. No ATVs.

5. Ride only vehicles the Owners have approved. Vehicles must be in safe working order, with working brakes and throttle, and sized for the rider.

6. No alcohol, cannabis, or impairing drugs or medication before or while riding.

7. Spectators stay behind the marked line, out of the riding area.

8. Ride jumps only after an Owner has cleared you for them.

9. Riding ends at dusk, or whenever an Owner says so.

10. Report every crash and every injury to an Owner right away.
